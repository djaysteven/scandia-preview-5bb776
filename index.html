FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Recovered from the hotel’s published FAQ page (live URL currently 404 — content from public index).

01What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in time is 2:00 pm. Check-out time is 12:00 noon.

02Do you have non-smoking rooms?

Yes — on request and subject to availability.

03Do you have twin, triple and quadruple rooms?

Only the Superior Twin Suite is twin-bedded. Other rooms can be converted to a triple with an extra bed. There are no quadruple rooms, but Deluxe Suites can stay 4 people.

04Do you have inter-connecting rooms?

Sorry, we don’t.

05What is the cost of an extra bed?

An extra bed is THB 150.

06Does the hotel offer baby-sitting?

Yes — book a day in advance. Cost is THB 250 per hour.

07Where is breakfast served and at what time?

Breakfast is served at the restaurant next to the lobby on the ground floor, from 8:00 am to 10:30 am.

08Do you have 24-hour room service?

No — room service is available from 9:00 am to 10:00 pm.

09Is there free parking?

Free public parking at Rompho Market near Scandia Beach Hotel.

10What is the length and depth of your pool?

In meters: 20 (length) × 3 (width) × 0.6 (depth).

11Do you have hairdryers / ironing?

Hairdryers in all rooms. Iron and ironing board are placed in the room.

12Is tap water safe to drink?

No — complimentary bottled water is supplied daily.

13Internet in rooms?

Wireless Fiber Internet. Complimentary for direct website bookings.

14Are dual telephone sockets available in rooms?

Dual telephone sockets are available only for the reception.
